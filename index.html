Happy Period Box — Curated Details

Explore what makes this curated collection deeply meaningful, comforting, and transformative.

ESSENTIALS & COMFORT

A Thoughtful Mix for Comfort & Relief

Created to acknowledge PMS, monthly fatigue, and the quiet pain that menstruators endure. A curated sanctuary of physical warmth, soothing sips, and magnesium-rich treats that validate her body's natural rhythm.

  • Soothing Botanical Tea Blend — Calming organic chamomile, peppermint & ginger infusion.
  • Herbal Cramp Relief Heat Comfort — Gentle warmth designed to ease abdominal tension and PMS discomfort.
  • Artisanal Mood-Lifting Dark Chocolate — Rich in magnesium, comforting, and consciously crafted.
THE PURPOSE & MEANING

Honoring the Rhythm of Her Body

Menstruation shouldn't be met with hush tones or hidden shame. The Happy Period Box is a tangible reminder that monthly cycles deserve comfort, understanding, and loving acknowledgment from those around us.

  • Validate Her Pain — Acknowledge PMS and fatigue with genuine warmth instead of dismissal.
  • Comfort & Rest — Provide physical warmth, soothing sips, and treats that calm the body.
  • Open the Dialogue — Normalize cycle conversations between partners, families, and friends.
WHAT'S INSIDE & PERSONAL TOUCH

Consciously Curated Self-Care & Support

Every box is assembled with intention and care. Alongside certified organic sanitary essentials, she receives gentle affirmation cards and a menstrual awareness guide reminding her that rest is not a luxury—it is essential.

  • Organic Period-Care Essentials — Gentle, skin-friendly sanitary care for delicate skin.
  • Affirmation & Menstrual Guide — Warm, validating words to remind her that rest is necessary.
  • Custom Handwritten Gift Note — Personalize with your heartfelt words of care and understanding.

THE LOVDARE INITIATIVE

Changing Conversations, One Gift at a Time.

Lovdare is an initiative that encourages us to love darefully and louder by transforming overlooked and often taboo topics into meaningful gifting experiences.

From menstruation and iron deficiency to postpartum wellbeing and menopause, our thoughtfully curated boxes aim to make conversations easier, encourage awareness, and remind women that their health and happiness matter.

Because care should never be limited to a particular age, stage, or occasion.
WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Guiding Pillars

Every box we curate is grounded in empathy, dignity, and the courage to show up for the women in our lives.

Break the Taboo

Encourage open conversations about topics that are often overlooked. We believe honesty and warmth replace stigma with genuine connection.

Carefully Curated

Thoughtful gifting that brings together comfort, awareness, and care. Every item is selected with intention to bring relief, warmth, and joy.

Care at Every Stage

Celebrate women's wellbeing through different phases of life. From early cycles to motherhood and graceful maturity, every milestone matters.

Make Care a Gift Worth Giving.
