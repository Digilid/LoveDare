EVERYDAY HEALTH & VITALITY

Gift Iron – Not Gold

1 in 2 Indian menstruators are anaemic. Anaemia is a silent epidemic that's affecting a huge population and symptoms are overlooked since they present as lack of concentration, tiredness, dizziness etc. Women are well known to ignore their own health and this issue gets overlooked most often till Pregnancy in the Indian scenarios. A collection of iron-rich products, snacks, cookies, and more is included to increase awareness and to prompt women to take care of their own health seriously. An educational booklet as well is included in the box. 1 in 2 Indian menstruators face anaemia, a silent epidemic often overlooked as everyday tiredness. This box pairs iron-rich snacks, cookies, and an educational booklet to help women prioritize their health.

MOTHERHOOD & FOURTH TRIMESTER

Post-Pregnancy Care Box

Till delivery, a woman is well taken care of throughout the entire pregnancy period. This attention shifts to the baby after delivery and the physical/mental health of the mother is overlooked. The post-delivery period is known as the 4th trimester because of the immense changes in hormonal levels and the psychological impacts of the same. This box was curated to shower love on the woman she is, not just as a mother, but as an independent human being who still deserves to be loved and cared for. It's made with self-care products to pamper her to ease the immense emotional roller coaster she goes through in the initial weeks post-delivery. Designed for the 4th trimester, when attention shifts to the baby and a mother's recovery is often overlooked. Curated with soothing self-care essentials to comfort and pamper her post-delivery.

MENOPAUSE JOURNEY & DIGNITY

Pre & Post-Menopausal Gift Box

Menopause as a topic is rarely discussed and the knowledge regarding the same is even scarce among doctors since it's dismissed just as a phase. Once you get older, the feeling of becoming invisible to the world gets stronger since your issues are no longer considered important. This menopause guided gift box is intended to address the immense changes happening in the body during this period, which most people find hard to process since it's all new and overwhelming. The phase of the end of fertility shouldn't make one feel smaller, instead be lovd and pampered as she has been all her life. Created to break the silence around menopause and support women through overwhelming hormonal changes. A guided gift box to remind her she deserves to feel seen, loved, and pampered at every stage.
